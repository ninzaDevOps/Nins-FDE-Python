❤️ Why We Rescue

Every day, animals are abandoned, injured or separated from their families. WildCare works with volunteers, veterinarians and animal lovers to provide emergency rescue, medical care, food, shelter and rehabilitation. Our ultimate goal is simple: rescue them, heal them and give them a better life.
